## Homepage decision

- Primary positioning: **Mandarin listening and speaking practice for intermediate learners**.
- HSK 3–4 remains a qualifier, not the main promise, because its SERP often skews toward exam preparation.
- Shadowing and dictation explain the method; they do not replace the homepage promise.
- The homepage CTA leads directly to the free lesson with no sign-up step.
